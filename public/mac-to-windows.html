Ferry

Share files between a Mac and a Windows PC

Macs and Windows PCs don't share AirDrop or Nearby Share. Ferry bridges them from the browser: open one page on both and drag files across, with no shared folders, SMB settings or USB stick.

Open Ferry Free, nothing to install. Open the same page on the other device.

How to do it

  1. Make sure both computers are on the same network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet on the same router).
  2. Open ferry.luitlabs.in on the Mac (Safari, Chrome or Firefox) and on the PC.
  3. Drag files onto the page, or click the other computer and choose files.
  4. Click Accept on the receiving computer. The file goes to its Downloads folder.

Big folders and videos

There's no size cap set by Ferry. On a computer running Chrome or Edge, files over 512 MB are written straight to disk as they arrive (you're asked where to save), so even multi-gigabyte videos are fine. More on large files.

Faster than a USB stick, often

On a 5 GHz network, transfers commonly run at tens of megabytes per second, and a computer on an Ethernet cable is faster still. There's no copy-to-stick, walk over, copy-off step.

Questions

Is it private? Do my files go through a server?

No. Files and text travel directly from one device to the other over your own network, encrypted in transit. Ferry's server only introduces the two devices to each other; it never sees, uploads or stores what you send.

Do both devices have to be on the same Wi-Fi?

Yes. Because nothing goes through a server, the two devices must be able to reach each other directly, which means the same Wi-Fi network (a computer on an Ethernet cable to the same router counts). Guest networks with device isolation block this.

How fast is it?

As fast as your Wi-Fi allows. On a 5 GHz network or Ethernet that is often tens of megabytes per second; on 2.4 GHz it's usually around 5 MB/s. Keeping both devices near the router helps.

My other device doesn't show up. What can I do?

Make sure both are on the same network and not on a VPN. Some routers (mesh systems, mixed IPv4/IPv6 setups) hide devices from each other; in that case tap “Don't see your device? Pair with a code” and type the 6-digit code on the other device.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

Ferry is free to use and there is no sign-up, no account and no app you have to install. Open the page and your device is ready.
