Ferry

Send files between devices on the same Wi-Fi. They travel straight from one device to the other — nothing is uploaded, nothing is kept.

On an iPhone

Open this page in Safari. It appears here as a device. Keep its tab in the foreground while sending, since iOS pauses background tabs.

On Android

Get the Ferry app from Google Play, then use Share → Ferry from Photos or Files.

On a computer

This page works in any browser. In Chrome, add Ferry for Chrome from the Chrome Web Store: it sits in Chrome's side panel and keeps the computer visible to your phone whenever Chrome is open, so you can send to it without opening a tab.

Why both devices must share a network

Transfers never pass through a server, so the two devices have to be able to reach each other directly. Guest Wi-Fi with device isolation blocks that.

Copy here, paste there

Tap Send text or a link (or just press Ctrl+V / ⌘V on this page), and it shows up on the other device with a Copy button. Like files, it goes straight between the devices.

Light or dark

The sun, moon and A buttons at the top pick light, dark, or automatic (follows your device).

Faster transfers

Speed is set by your Wi-Fi, not by Ferry. Connect both devices to your router's 5 GHz network (often named with "5G") — usually 3–5× faster than 2.4 GHz — and keep them near the router. A computer on an Ethernet cable is faster still.
